Artificial Intelligence is exploring new frontiers and has made strides in every industry sector. One of such sectors where the effect of AI has been felt is the Cybersecurity sector. Threat actors are using Artificial Intelligence to create new exploits and attack vectors all the while taking advantage of the shortcomings of current and legacy defense solutions used by thousands of organizations worldwide. What this means is that traditional defense measures are not keeping up with current attack techniques, allowing for a huge pool for attackers to come in and exploit.

Instead of out-right and immediate attacks, more adversaries are opting for more patient and stealthier attack techniques, often only executing their attack after several weeks, months, or even years of lying in wait undetected. A prime example of this is the 2020 Solarwinds SUNBURST Supply Chain Attack which lay in wait for 12 - 14 days to trigger it's exploit after the malicious modified software was installed by clients. This lead to an estimated $90 million in insured losses by companies.

However, all is not lost. A consistent pattern with all malware that attempts to hide itself, is that it always acts in suspicious ways and provides logs which would indicate such. An example would be a brand-new connection from the internal environment to an external service even though that internal environment should never make any connections to the wider Internet.

This is where Real-Time Anomaly Detection systems enhaced by Machine Learning come in to play.

These systems break away from the traditional models. Older models required several months of stored logs in order to create a baseline and then judge future events against that baseline. However, these systems are incredibly rigid, failing to account for major legitimate changes and can be poisoned from the start which means that similar intrusion attempts will not be flagged as anomalies later on.
This system is designed from the ground-up to work and adapt continuously. It is always fed with new logs and consistently re-evaluating it's baseline in order to flag anomalous events immediately. With Machine Learning capabilities it is meant to learn and understand the context surrounding each event and once it has matured, it should take decisions and execute certain permitted actions on it's own autonommy instead of the need for a Human Operator to deal with every single alert.

The risk of having the baseline poisoned is immediately eliminated due to the real-time nature. It is constantly learning from the environment, and with big context windows it is will still detect any potential attempts at manipulating the baseline.

ML Enhanced Real-Time Anomaly Detection systems are steps above traditional infrastructure, and is a solution designed specifically to leverage both the raise in AI and the new techniques attackers are using to gain an advantage over their attackers.
